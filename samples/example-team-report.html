Scope. Pull requests only. Nothing here sees design work, mentoring, on-call, incident response, support load, or whether the team was pointed at the right problem. A quarter's PR count is not a quarter's contribution.

How team rates are built. Every rate is recomputed from the pooled counts across all members, never averaged from individual rates — averaging would weight a member with 2 PRs the same as one with 20. Cycle-time figures come from the full pooled list of merged PRs.

Handling. Contains per-person metrics for identifiable colleagues. Keep it out of shared drives and version control. Where you are subject to works-council or co-determination rules, systematic individual productivity data usually needs to be agreed before it is collected.
